Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact NextUs, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, business or organization, email address, selected service, and project details.

Our website may also collect limited technical and usage information, including pages viewed, links or buttons clicked, timestamps, referring pages, language preference, and general browser or device information.

2. Why We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, discuss and provide services, communicate about projects, and maintain business records.

We use website usage information to understand how visitors use the site, improve navigation and content, measure which areas are useful, and identify technical problems.

3. Analytics and Click Tracking

NextUs uses first-party event tracking to record limited website interactions. Examples include page views and clicks on navigation links, service links, pricing links, and project CTAs.

The tracking payload is designed not to include contact-form contents, passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information. We do not intentionally track the text entered into form fields.

Analytics requests may be processed by our hosting infrastructure. Server providers may also create ordinary technical logs such as request timestamps and IP addresses as part of operating and securing the service.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NextUs may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies for language preferences, basic website functionality, and analytics. A browser identifier may be treated as personal-related information under Japanese privacy guidance depending on how it is handled and combined with other information.

5. Contact Form

Information submitted through the contact form is processed through our email service to deliver the inquiry to NextUs. We use that information only for responding to the inquiry and related project communication, subject to applicable law.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services such as hosting, email delivery, fonts, APIs, or other infrastructure. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requests relating to access, correction, deletion, or use of information. Contact us through the contact page for privacy-related requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, tracking methods, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with the updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the NextUs contact page.
